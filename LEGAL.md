- The original copyright messages inside the ROM files must **not** be altered.
- A note must be included stating that Amstrad has granted permission for the

- No fee may be charged for the ROM images themselves (a fee may be charged for
  the emulator code).

The required attribution statement is:

> Amstrad have kindly given their permission for the redistribution of their
> copyrighted material but retain that copyright.

## Locomotive Software (BASIC ROM)

The BASIC ROM was written by Locomotive Software and is their copyright, not

**non-profit** use only — any charge may only cover distribution costs. This
project is free and open source, and no charge is made for the ROM images, so
it falls within that allowance.
